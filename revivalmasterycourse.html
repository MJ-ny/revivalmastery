The German Level System

A Levels — Foundation Stage
Core grammar and everyday communication skills.

B Levels — Independence Stage
Real conversation ability and work or study readiness.

C Levels — Mastery Stage
Academic and professional fluency.

Progress principle: A1 to B1 builds grammar and structural foundation. B2 to C2 develops fluency, accuracy, and expression control.

One-to-One Training: Duration is customized after demo assessment based on learner pace and goals.

A1 — Beginner Foundation

Goal: Build the base and begin speaking basic German with confidence.

  • Introductions and greetings
  • Numbers, time, and dates
  • Daily needs and shopping language
  • Essential everyday phrases
  • Core beginner vocabulary set

Grammar Focus: Present tense, basic sentence structure, article system.

Outcome: Ability to manage simple everyday conversations.

A2 — Elementary Communication

Goal: Handle routine communication situations independently.

  • Daily routines and activities
  • Travel and services
  • Personal experiences and descriptions
  • Common situation dialogues
  • Expanded vocabulary base

Grammar Focus: Past tense basics, modal verbs, connectors.

Outcome: Communicate in common real-life contexts.

B1 — Independent User

Goal: Operate independently in German across daily life, study, and work contexts.

  • Work and job topics
  • Housing and administration
  • Opinions and explanations
  • Structured speaking tasks

Grammar Focus: Reflexive verbs, Konjunktiv II basics, complex sentences.

Outcome: Independent functional communication ability.

B2 — Upper Intermediate

Goal: Achieve professional and academic readiness.

  • Academic vocabulary
  • Workplace communication
  • Discussions and presentations
  • Argument development

Outcome: Meets many university and professional language requirements.

C1 — Advanced Fluency

Goal: Express complex ideas clearly and naturally.

  • Academic discussions
  • Abstract and technical topics
  • Style and tone control
  • Precision speaking

Outcome: University-level fluency.

C2 — Mastery Level

Goal: Near-native command of German.

  • Understanding complex texts and speech
  • Handling technical material
  • Expressing subtle meaning differences
  • Adapting speaking style to context

Outcome: Expert-level German proficiency.
