Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join Revival Mastery classes?

Our classes are suitable for school students, college students, working professionals, and anyone looking to improve academics, language skills, or communication abilities.

Do you provide a free demo class?

Yes. We provide a free demo class so you can understand our teaching style, course structure, and interaction before enrolling.

Are the classes online or offline?

We provide both online classes and home tuition, allowing students from anywhere in India or abroad to learn comfortably.

What learning support and courses do you provide?

We provide complete learning support including language classes, public speaking training, school and college subjects, assignments, project work, exam preparation, and home tuition.

How long does it take to complete a course?

The course duration depends on the subject, student level, and learning goals. Timelines vary for language classes, academics, public speaking, and project support, and are planned based on individual needs.
