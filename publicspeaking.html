    GOOD TO KNOW

    Questions, answered.

    A few quick details before you begin.

    Is the practice tool free?

    Yes, this first version is free to try, with a small daily practice limit in the same browser.

    Is this a full AI speaking coach?

    Not yet. This first version checks the transcript for word count, estimated pace and common filler words. It does not yet assess pronunciation, vocal tone or body language using AI.

    What if my microphone does not work?

    Allow microphone access and use a supported browser. You can also type or paste your speech into the transcript box and review it.

    Can I get personal coaching too?

    Yes. Explore Revival Mastery's programmes and book a free demo through our main website.
