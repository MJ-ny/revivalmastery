German Levels for Germany (A1–C2)

A1 – Beginner

Foundation Communication Program

Build a strong foundation in German. Learn pronunciation, daily sentences, introductions and basic communication with confidence.

A2 – Elementary

Conversation & Grammar Expansion

Improve fluency for everyday situations. Focus on real-life conversations, sentence building and grammar clarity.

B1 – Intermediate

Exam-Ready Communication Level

Strong focus on speaking, writing, listening and reading with preparation for Goethe / TELC certification.

B2 – Upper Intermediate

Professional & Academic German

Use German confidently for work and studies. Advanced grammar, discussions and fluent communication skills.

C1 – Advanced

High-Level Professional Fluency

Master complex language structures. Focus on presentations, debates, academic texts and professional communication.

C2 – Proficiency

Near-Native Mastery

Achieve precise, confident and natural expression. Advanced vocabulary, nuance and near-native level fluency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join Revival Mastery German classes?

Our German classes are suitable for school students, college students, working professionals, and Germany aspirants. You can join from beginner (A1) to advanced (C2) level.

Do you provide a free demo class?

Yes. We provide a free demo class so you can understand our teaching style, course structure, and interaction before enrolling.

Are the German classes online or offline?

All our German classes are conducted online, allowing students from anywhere in India or abroad to join comfortably.

Which German exams do you prepare students for?

We prepare students for Goethe, TELC, and other internationally recognised German language examinations with exam-focused training.

How long does it take to complete one German level?

On average, each level takes around 6 to 8 weeks depending on the student’s pace, practice consistency, and class frequency.
